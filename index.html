♠Pokerly
Editing a saved game. Changes save to History automatically. Your current game in the other tab isn't affected. Close this tab when you're done.

After each hand, click Winner, pick who won (or everyone who tied), and enter what everyone else lost. It goes into the winners' columns for you, split equally on a tie. Each cell is what the row player owes the column player, and you can still type into cells directly. Type an amount and press Enter: it's added and you jump to the cell below, then to the top of the next column. Press Enter on an empty cell to skip it, or Shift+Enter to go back up. You can also type 10+200+30 to add several at once. Click an amount to remove it. Ctrl/⌘+Z undoes.
